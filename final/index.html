Welcome to The Hip-Hop Dance Museum

The Hip-Hop Dance Museum is the world's first museum dedicated entirely to the art, culture, and history of hip-hop dance. Located in the Bronx — the birthplace of hip-hop — we celebrate the movement that changed the world, from breaking and popping to locking, krump, and beyond.

Whether you're a lifelong dancer, a cultural historian, or a first-time visitor, our exhibitions offer an immersive journey through five decades of hip-hop movement. Explore interactive installations, rare archival footage, and live performance spaces that honor the pioneers and innovators who built this culture from the ground up. Plan your visit and be part of the story.
