Explore the Museum

The Hip-Hop Dance Museum was founded in 2016 with a single mission: to preserve, celebrate, and share the legacy of hip-hop dance with the world. Rooted in the Bronx — where it all began — we are a living archive of movement, culture, and community.

  • Founded in 2016 in the South Bronx, New York City
  • Dedicated entirely to hip-hop dance history and culture
  • Home to 5 permanent galleries and rotating international exhibitions
  • Features live performance spaces, archival footage, and interactive installations
  • Welcomes over 200,000 visitors annually
